How do I get here from the airport?

It's 6 km, about 15 minutes by Grab or taxi. Show the driver 252 Dương Đình Nghệ, An Hải Bắc, Sơn Trà.

How do I pay?

At the property, by cash or card (including JCB and UnionPay). There's no damage deposit. Bring photo ID; the lead guest must be 18 or over.

Can I stay for a month?

Yes. The apartments are also rented long-term. Message the host on WhatsApp or Zalo with your dates for a monthly rate.

Is breakfast included?

No. The coffee house is downstairs, most apartments have a full kitchen, and grocery delivery is available.

Is there parking and laundry?

Parking is free. Most apartments have a washing machine, and there's a washer and dryer on the roof.

Children, pets, smoking?

Children of all ages are welcome. No pets and no parties. The building is non-smoking, with a designated smoking area.
