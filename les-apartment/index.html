A day from Le's

One way to spend it, all from the same front door.

05:30

Sunrise at My Khe

My Khe faces east, so the day starts over the water. Locals swim before the heat, and the round basket boats come in with the night's catch.

19 minutes on foot

07:00

Coffee downstairs

Back home, the coffee house on the ground floor is open. Order a cà phê sữa đá: strong phin coffee, condensed milk, a glass of ice.

“The coffee shop downstairs is nice, cozy and the coffee is great.”

One floor down

08:00

Mì Quảng for breakfast

Turmeric noodles in a little rich broth, with peanuts and a rice cracker to crumble over. It's Quảng Nam's dish, and Đà Nẵng eats it at every hour. Bún chả cá, a fish-cake noodle soup, is the other local breakfast.

10:00

Up the Sơn Trà peninsula

The 67-metre Lady Buddha at Linh Ứng Pagoda looks back over the bay and the whole city. Higher up, the forest is home to the red-shanked douc langur.

About 20 minutes by Grab

14:00

The hot hours, at home

Đà Nẵng slows down after lunch, and so can you. The air conditioning is on, there's a kitchen and fridge for cut fruit, and most apartments have their own washing machine for the beach towels.

See the six apartments
17:30

The Han River at golden hour

Walk west to the riverside promenade as the bridges switch on. The Love Lock Bridge and the carp-dragon statue are just upstream of the Dragon Bridge.

10 to 14 minutes on foot

21:00

The river lights up

Every bridge over the Han is lit at night. On Saturdays and Sundays at nine, the Dragon Bridge's golden head breathes fire, then water. The Sơn Trà night market is at the bridge's foot, with grilled seafood and sugarcane juice.

15 minutes on foot

Is breakfast included?

No. The coffee house is downstairs, most apartments have a full kitchen, and grocery delivery is available.

What do I need at check-in?

Photo ID and a card. The lead guest must be 18 or over. Express and contactless check-in are available.

Children, pets, smoking?

Children of all ages are welcome. No pets and no parties. The building is non-smoking, with a designated smoking area.

Which languages does the host speak?

English, French, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Chinese and Vietnamese.
