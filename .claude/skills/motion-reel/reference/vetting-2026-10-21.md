## Repo notes

**charlie947.** This is the only new explainer-specific skill in the batch. `vox-explainer` look: torn paper, halftone, paper and

`downloads/*.zip` are duplicates of those two SKILL.md files.

**howseen.** Nothing new since the last vet. Its §8 critique scoring, §9b silent "one sentence" test, §9c judge ≠ builder and
§9d "mascot SVG narrator" are still the useful parts.

**whaleyxbt.** Same as before. `sound-design` has the event→sound table, the gain hierarchy, pan follows x, one key, and a pitch
ladder. `review-loop` makes beat sheets 0.3 s after each settle. `web-sims` adds "chapters + one-line caption per step + labels

**remotion-dev/skills.** Still 12 skills. For us the useful part is `remotion-markup/text-highlights.md` (rough-notation

**LottieFiles.** Borrow two rules from `skills/motion-design/director/choreography.md`. The 1/3 rule: with 3+ animated elements,

**awesome-opus5-5-videos.** A gallery of prompts, not code. Most value is in the ~10 long, structured prompts. Most "explainer"
entries are one-liners with no reusable technique. See "Best examples" below.

**threeui. What it is:** "ThreeUI Community", Meng To's open-source edition of threeui.com, a catalogue of about 50
WebGL/Three.js/Canvas hero backgrounds, buttons, landing pages and effects. It is a React/Vite site and an npm package
(`@designcodeio/threeui`). Pro components need an OAuth CLI. **Is it relevant to video?** Only loosely. Every scene runs on wall
clock time (rAF/`performance.now`) and many accumulate state or use unseeded `Math.random`. That means it can't be seeked
frame-exact, and it would need time virtualisation, PRNG seeding and step-from-zero rendering in our headless pipeline.
Fragment-shader-only pieces are the exception: they set a `u_time` uniform from the clock, so `u_time = f(t)` makes them
seekable. Examples: `src/shaders/stream-convergence/StreamConvergenceBackground.tsx` (`u_time = now*0.0003*speed`) and the
topography and halftone flow fields. Even those pull CDN scripts and need SwiftShader/ANGLE flags for headless WebGL. They are
built as website heroes, pointer-reactive and often with tech-brand marks. **Use:** none directly. Some visuals are worth
re-implementing in our `draw(t)` as metaphors: see the "Abstract / metaphor visuals" section below.

## Techniques to borrow for educational explainers

House-rule check first: motion-reel `SKILL.md` line 62 says a character or mascot comes "only from a supplied asset". Most
of the explainer examples below rely on one simple original character, such as a microbe or a fibre strand drawn in code. That
needs a deliberate exception for non-brand explainers: an original geometric character, labelled as illustration, never a real
person or brand. Decide this before borrowing the character items.

### Story and scene grammar
- **Brief = question + visual turn + metaphor**: "[question]? And then [the unexpected on-screen turn]. [What the idea looks
  like]." (`charlie947 prompts/briefs.md`) Gut example: "Why does fibre matter? And then we shrink into the colon, where it
  becomes dinner for trillions of microbes."
- **Script rules**: 8–14 shots, one sentence each, shot 1 is the question, each shot answers something the previous line
  raised, and the last shot is the takeaway in ≤ 5 words. Show the numbered script and wait for a yes.
  (`charlie947 skills/vox-explainer/SKILL.md` Step 2)
- **Shot vocabulary for the shotlist** (`vox-explainer` Step 3):
  - kinetic statement (3 short lines on a strip)
  - cards (several things at once)
  - before/after (same card, the date chip flips)
  - label call-out (a marker circle draws itself, then the label)
  - counter (computed, never guessed)
  - grid (many identical tiles, then one breaks the pattern)

  Add these to `templates/shotlist.md` as shot types.
- **Content contract**: one core question, a curiosity hook, a counter-intuitive point, everyday analogies. Facts from original
  papers, institutions or textbooks, never marketing accounts. (`awesome…/prompts/cryptoxorz-359201.md`) Hedge wording:
  "looks like", not "is"; "one reason", not "the reason". (`vox-explainer` review rules and "will not do")
- **Hook**: say the question big. The first surprise lands within 5 s. A small title that undersells the film loses people.
  (`vox-explainer` review rules; `prompts/briefs.md` note "the text at the start undersells what's coming next")
- **Storyboard table columns**: time, what's on screen, what the moment is for, how it leaves, which object carries into the
  next shot. Aim for about 12–15 compositions per 30 s at 1.4–3.5 s each, or about 22–27 for 50 s. Name three signature
  moments without using effect names. Vary scale (close, wide, overhead, full-frame type) and never repeat the "heading over
  three cards" layout. Every action produces a visible result. (`awesome…` `everestchris6-399895`, `<storyboard>` and
  `<motion_principles>`)
- **Wireframe-first**: build each beat as boxes, outlined text and guide lines with no colour, lock timing on one still per
  beat, then skin from the design tokens without moving the layout. Render final | wireframe side by side for review.
  (`awesome…` `archiveexplorer-560622`) This fits motion-reel's timeline file well as a `--wireframe` render flag.
- **Mechanism equals argument**: every on-screen action must correspond to one step of the reasoning ("the mechanism must
  demonstrate the proof rather than decorate a narrated explanation"). Write explicit logic guardrails into the brief ("do not
  claim X always…"). (`awesome…` `gdgtify-393073`, Euclid proof) For gut: fibre in → microbes ferment → short-chain fatty
  acids → gut lining, each one visible cause-and-effect beat.
- **Simulate the real thing**: an actual random walk, real counts (1 marble = 1 M people), computed images rather than
  drawings. Precompute the simulation once at load with a seed, and have `render(t)` only read it. (`astrothewizard-618782`,
  `rneayan-417829`, `cagrimbakirci-842869` in `awesome…`; `whaleyxbt .claude/skills/web-sims/SKILL.md`)
- **Scale journeys**: whole body → organ → tissue → cell → molecule as one continuous zoom. (`curieuxexplorer-016717`, T1D
  "from the whole body down to a single molecule". `kgonia7-746268` and `acoramaa-053577` are powers-of-ten zooms.
  `nft-chen-068069` holds 15 ten-second educational micro-briefs: Kurzgesagt paper-fold, real-radius scale comparison,
  3b1b circle-area rearrangement, whiteboard, isometric process, picture-book.) Zoom in log space; motion-reel already has it.
- **Metaphor sentence that carries the whole film**: "The fuel is still there. The message is missing."
  (`curieuxexplorer-016717`) Compare states on the same input: Healthy vs condition vs treated, same meal.
  End with "Educational visualization, not medical advice."
- **One persistent object/ink across scenes**: e.g. "one pencil line gets promoted three times" (graphite → blueprint wire →
  light), with each stage's accent becoming the next stage's colour. Every handoff starts on the exact last frame of the
  previous scene; draw the outgoing scene under the transition; scenes sharing an object share one zoom function; a flood
  still retreating when the next scene starts reads as a hard cut. (`heyiamnick-119558`, `twoclipping-041659` `<gotchas>`)

### Kinetic typography rhythm
- **Words get physical roles**. Example: WAIT is a lintel, WAITING a suspended load, VOICE a support, ROOM an opening, FLOOR a
  platform. The roles come from the letterforms themselves, not from illustrations behind them. Reveal phrase groups in
  reading order. Cue on phrases, pauses and emphasis, not a dance beat. Hold ≥ 400 ms of absolute stillness once, before the
  resolution. Settle loads with critically damped motion; only one element (the baseline) gets elastic deformation. A phrase
  stays readable before it turns into architecture. Transition between unrelated words through occlusion or matched edges,
  never arbitrary path interpolation. (`awesome…` `gdgtify-929495`, `gdgtify-130294`, `gdgtify-989608`) Gut use: FIBRE
  breaks into food particles that microbes eat; BARRIER letters tighten into a wall.
- **Critically damped spring** `1 − (1 + ωt)·e^(−ωt)`, ω 12–40, never overshoots. Moves start 0.05 s before their beat so
  they land on it. (`heyiamnick-119558` `<structure>`)
- **Caption discipline**: ≤ 7 words, one idea per shot, hand-placed line breaks, one text layer on screen at a time. Words go
  grey → white over 0.2 s on the word timestamps. Readable things stay ≥ 8 clean frames. Glitches only on a real cut, 1–2
  frames, always with a click. (`notdwd-998633` `<build>` 4–5)
- **One accent per line**: a single italic or accent-coloured word carries the point. Mono labels are decoration; anything the
  viewer must read goes big. (`whaleyxbt .claude/skills/motion-design/SKILL.md` §5)
- **Elastic type / masked type** recipes: letters change width as well as height on a locked baseline, and only the middle
  letter takes the accent. Artwork rises inside the letterforms, with a marker line showing the fill edge.
  (`charlie947 skills/motion-effects/references/effects.md` #09, #10)
- **Headline reflow**: the same text travels to its new layout and is never retyped. Everything else arrives in reading order.
  (`effects.md` #11 Text → layout)

### Diagram animation
- **Rough annotations driven by progress**: highlight, circle, underline, strike, bracket and box, drawn on with a 0–1
  progress value from the timeline. (`remotion-dev/skills skills/remotion-markup/text-highlights.md`, @remotion/rough-notation)
  Re-implement in `lib/motion.js` as seeded rough strokes; don't add the dependency. The vox label call-out ("marker circle
  draws itself, then a label") is the same move.
- **Flowing paths**: pulses leave a source with a 0.14 s stagger and draw the connector as they travel (the line is never
  switched on). Each node answers with an expanding ring on arrival, and a second wave keeps the hold alive. Lines shade from
  cool to accent on the second leg. (`effects.md` #15) Gut use: food → colon → microbes → metabolites → bloodstream, or the
  gut–brain axis.
- **Perspective shift**: flat layers separate back to front toward one shared vanishing point, and each layer keeps real
  content. (`effects.md` #13) Gut use: mucus layer / epithelium / immune cells.
- **Chart morph**: the same values become bars, then a line through the bar tops. Values never change; the line is drawn, not
  faded; the last point takes the accent. Leave a ghost of the start state. (`effects.md` #05 and "What I learned")
- **Exploded labelled view**: parts slide apart, each with its name. Labels are drawn in screen space from projected
  positions, never as text inside 3D. Use 3D only where it explains something physical or spatial. (`aichrislee-378670`
  heart; `everestchris6-399895` `<3d>`)
- **Grid with one breaker**: many identical tiles, then one breaks the pattern. (`vox-explainer` Grid shot) Gut use: one
  microbe species out of balance.

### Character and illustration
- **Pencil/boil look**:
  - Resample each path and push it off true with slow sines whose phase changes 8×/s (line boil on eights).
  - Stroke twice: full width at 90 % opacity, then half width at 40 % with more wobble. Knock paper tooth out of the strokes
    and multiply onto paper.
  - Fill with hatching drawn one line at a time. Type can be hatching cut to the letter shapes, with a doubled outline.

  Sources: `heyiamnick-119558` "Three inks". `whaleyxbt motion-design` §7: grain reseeded on twos, line boil faded out as
  fidelity rises (a nice sketch → finished device for "understanding grows"). `vox-explainer`: motion on twos. Candidate
  `lib/motion.js` helpers: `boil(points, t, {rate:8})`, `pencil(ctx, path, t)`, `hatch(ctx, shape, angle, progress)`.
- **Simple original character spec**: define it in units (body 76×54 rounded, arm stubs 24×16, legs 11×33, two tall
  rounded-rect eyes). Draw it in whatever ink the scene uses, so it is never an extra colour. Run cycle: alternate leg pairs at
  6 steps/s, leaning 7° into the run. (`heyiamnick-119558` `<mascot>`) "One simple original character" plus 3 ideas, each with
  a caption taken from the source text. (`woojinrad-455731`) Give the subject a personality with visual gags, onomatopoeia
  and callbacks. (`astrothewizard-618782`) A mascot narrator carries the story. (`howseen skill/motion-design/SKILL.md` §9d)
- **Texture stack**: torn-paper cards with small shadows, halftone, paper grain plus fine film grain, colour fringing at the
  frame edges only, soft vignette. (`vox-explainer` Step 4 build brief) Halation on bright edges, warm lifted blacks, fresh
  grain every frame. (`notdwd-998633`)

### Review loops
- **A fresh critic that never saw the build**. Never tell it what you think you fixed. It pulls its own frames (sheet every
  0.2 s plus dense frames around transitions), measures, and returns a ranked list ending in "ship" or "one more pass". The
  next critic marks each earlier item fixed, partly fixed or still there. Keep a ledger of rounds with before/after numbers.
  (`everestchris6-399895` `<critic_loop>`) Two reviewers rank P0/P1/P2 and a third fixes: every P0 gets fixed, P1/P2 only if
  worth it, back up before each round and roll back what can't be fixed. (`voxyz-ai-345550`) Judge once; only egregious items
  go back, with no blanket redo loops. (`stokebuilder-356793`)
- **Anti-invented-revisions prompt**: "Report only problems you actually observe… If no problems are found, say so and do not
  invent a revision." (`charlie947 skills/animated-chart/SKILL.md` Prompt 2) Add this line to `prompts/critique-pass.md`.
- **Notes one at a time**: "Fix one thing only… keep the motion, the timing… tell me what you changed." Notes describe how it
  feels, not how to fix it. (`charlie947 prompts/polish.md`, `prompts/briefs.md` "How to give a note")
- **Measured gates**:
  - ≤ ~1 s of frozen screen per 30 s; holds keep a micro-life (a pulse, a second wave), never a dead still.
  - Frame 0 is a finished picture.
  - Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1.
  - Nothing collides with or flies through text.
  - A first-time viewer gets it with sound off, and a fresh agent can restate the message from frames alone.

  (`everestchris6-399895` `<quality_bar>`; `howseen` §9b "silent one-sentence test", §9c)
- **Recipe self-checks**: frames at the start, during the move and at the end of the move must all differ, or part of the
  effect isn't running. Pause on the hold frame: the point must read from that single still. Load every font before `seek(0)`,
  because frame 0 rendered in a fallback font. (`effects`/`motion-effects SKILL.md` Step 4 and "What I learned")
- **Editor's check for facts**: every on-screen name, date and number matches the facts file character for character.
  (`vox-explainer` Step 5) Keep a `facts.md` (number + source + date); no facts file means no numbers on screen.
  (`howseen` §9b)

### Sound
- **Event → sound map** (`whaleyxbt .claude/skills/sound-design/SKILL.md` "Visual event → sound"; previously borrowed in part,
  these are the parts still missing):
  - a `scratch` texture for every draw-on or annotation
  - pops whose pitch rises across a sequence
  - one riser per video that ends exactly on the hit
  - a chime chord in key on the payoff
  - one impact per video
  - pan follows on-screen x
  - every tonal sound in one key, with pitch rising with progress and resolving on the payoff
  - continuous motion stays silent
- **VO first**: the voice sits on top; if you can't hear it, drop the score. A hit on each new shot and a lift on the last
  line. (`vox-explainer` review rules) Narrator 10–15 dB over the music, ducked −18 dB while speaking, hits on the exact action
  frame, −14 LUFS. If the narrator pauses, the visuals must not stop. (`notdwd-998633` `<build>` 7, `<gotchas>`)
- **Score tracks the story state**: the score changes with each scene. (`astrothewizard-618782`) Music as the clock:
  instrumentation and tempo evolve with the narrative and every cut lands on the beat. (`dotey-927166`) Gut use: a thin,
  dissonant bed under "imbalance", resolving to a fuller chord on "balance".
