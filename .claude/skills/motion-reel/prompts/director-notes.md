# Giving and taking director notes

Good notes give a timestamp, a verb and a reason. Turn each note into a one-line edit in `timeline.json` or one
function. Then re-render only the affected seconds as stills and show them before re-rendering video.

| Vague | Director note |
|---|---|
| "make it more dynamic" | "2.0–4.5 s: the field types too slowly. Finish typing by beat 6 and let the answer land on the downbeat at 4.0." |
| "the map is weak" | "7.0–9.5 s: the province reads as a grey blob at phone size. Fill it with ink from Montréal outward over 2 beats." |
| "too busy" | "12.0 s: tabs and counter both move. Hold the counter still until the indicator settles." |
| "feels AI-made" | "Remove the corner wordmark and the frame border. Nothing lives in the corners." |
| "sound is off" | "Every click is ~80 ms late against the cursor press. Move the cues to the press frame." |

Taking notes:
- Repeat back what you will change and what stays. Don't touch what wasn't mentioned.
- If a note conflicts with a hard rule (real UI only, no invented data, Quebec French), say so and offer the closest
  compliant alternative.
- When the user says it's enough ("enough for the renders"), stop iterating and ship what you have.
