Root causes, ranked by impact

#1

Measurement is broken end to end, so every optimisation is blind

The app has no tags at all. Meta is told a CTA click is a "registration", Google Ads has no conversions, UTMs are dropped on the way into the app (62% of sign-ups have no source), and QA traffic and two promo codes add noise. Impact: Meta's algorithm is learning to find people who click buttons, and nobody can say which dollars produced the 7 paying teams. This also causes #2.

#2

Budget spread too thin, on the wrong inventory

11 Google campaigns and 8 Meta ad sets on $13.5/day is ≈ $0.71/day per unit. A Meta ad set needs ~50 optimisation events a week to exit learning, so none of them can learn. Broad match bought brand searches, a Display "Institutions" campaign targets the wrong customer (teams of 5–25), and Audience Network bought junk clicks. 34% of all spend went to the US ($31.6 CPM) for 0 sign-ups. 82% of Meta spend went to ad sets with 0 recorded sign-ups.

#3

No path from payment to "invite 2 teammates"

7 teams paid and 0 activated. After checkout the product shows 4 overlays and an empty Space. There is no Invite button in the sidebar, although the landing-page mock shows one, and the banner offers "invite up to 4 teammates" to a customer profile of 5–25 people. This is the biggest threat to month-2 renewals, but #1 has to be fixed before we can even measure it.

What's working: keep it

  • The offer converts: 24% of sign-ups pay $1, even with a card required.
  • Cost per paying team is $17.4 against a $31 target, even on this messy traffic.
  • Clean traffic converts on the landing page at ~11%, so keep the page and fix the traffic and the handoff.
  • PH Interest: $5.70 bought 45 landing page views and 5 sign-ups ($1.14 per sign-up). "Meta SEA" is the largest real source in the backend (15 clicks).
  • The "Teams" search theme (SEA Teams had the most clicks): keep the theme, rebuild the keywords.
  • LinkedIn KOC posts: 116.5 engagements per post, 2× Facebook partner pages and 7× TikTok. This audience fits B2B, so keep them with a unique code each.
  • The foundation exists: GTM, GA4, the Google Ads tag, the pixel and a cta_click event are already on the landing page.

Stop

  • The US test (34% of spend, 0 sign-ups, $31.6 CPM)
  • Display, Audience Network and broad match without negatives
  • Splitting $13.5/day across 19 campaigns and ad sets
  • Judging channels on CTR or pixel "sign-ups"

Part 3 · Experiments
5/6

Three experiments, each judged by a metric closer to revenue than CTR.

E1 · Landing page → sign-up (now 5.8%)
Hypothesis

Cold paid visitors drop off because the hero sells "workflow" in the abstract, there's no product on the mobile first screen, and the CTA opens "Welcome back · Sign in". Showing the outcome for their job, plus one-click account creation, will lift sign-ups.

Change

Fix first, to 100% of traffic with no test: the CTA opens "Create your team" with Google/Microsoft SSO first.
Then A/B the hero. Control: "Lead, run your team workflow for $1". Variant: "One Space per client: files, chat and tasks together", with real product UI above the fold on mobile, a risk-reversal line under the button ("Reminder email 3 days before renewal · cancel in one click") and a trust row (Built in Europe · Swiss company · GDPR). No invented logos or numbers.

Metric

Server-side sign-ups ÷ unique landing page visitors. Guardrail: sign-up → paying.

Sample / run time

8% → 11.2% (+40% relative) at 80% power and α 0.05 needs ~1,330 visitors per arm. Split 50/50, sticky by visitor: ≈ 8–9 days of Meta + Google traffic.

Win / loss

Win: ≥ +25% relative, p < 0.05, and sign-up → paying no more than 3 points lower. Loss: no lift at the full sample, so keep the control and test the offer block next.

E2 · Paying $1 → activated (now 0%)
Hypothesis

Teams pay, but nothing leads them to invite anyone. If "invite 2 teammates" is the first screen after payment, and is framed as the point of the $1, activation will rise.

Change

After checkout, a full-screen 2-step setup: (1) name your Space, pre-filled from the onboarding answers ("Client: …"); (2) invite 2 teammates by email fields, a copyable invite link, or sharing to WhatsApp, Viber or Slack. Suppress the 4 overlays in the first session. Add a permanent Invite button to the sidebar. Day 0/1/3 email and in-app nudge to the payer: "Your Space is ready, 2 seats are waiting".

Metric

Share of paying teams with a Space and ≥ 2 invites within 72 h. Secondary: invite acceptance rate, members active on day 7.

Sample / run time

50/50 split by team. Detecting 30% → 60% needs ~42 teams per arm, ≈ 4 weeks at ~20 paying teams a week. Read early with a Bayesian check: P(variant is better) ≥ 95%.

Win / loss

Win: ≥ +20 points of activation and ≥ 95% probability. Loss: less than +10 points after 84 teams, so test an invite incentive (an extra seat for a third invite) next.

E3 · My choice: creative angle → cost per paying team
Hypothesis

Angle B, "Share with control", attracts agencies that really share files with clients, so it brings in paying teams more cheaply than the $1 offer (C), even with a lower CTR. Judging creative on CTR would pick the wrong winner.

Change

3 angles (A before/after · B client control · C $1 offer) × 2 formats inside the single PH ad set. Same CTA, same landing page, copy of the same length, and utm_content per ad, read in the backend.

Metric

Cost per paying team per angle from the backend. Secondary: sign-up → paying and CTR.

Sample / run time

7 days, with each angle reaching ≥ $60 spend or ≥ 20 sign-ups. Meta doesn't split budget evenly, so if an angle has spent less than $40 by D14, move it into a Meta A/B test.

Win / loss

Win: the angle with the lowest cost per paying team, at least 30% better than #2. Make 3 new hooks on it. Tie or loss: keep both angles and test the persona next (agency vs remote team).

Part 4 · Product experience — landing page → invite a teammate (my walkthrough, 25 Nov)
6/6

Activation requires 2 invites, yet inviting is the hardest action to find.

1 · A new paid visitor lands on "Welcome back · Sign in"

The CTA points to /settings?…step=checkout, so logged-out visitors are bounced to the login page, where "Create one" is a small link at the bottom. The referral link (?ref=WR5HX8) also redirects to login and loses both the ref code and the UTMs.
Fix: open "Create your team" with Google/Microsoft SSO first, and carry the offer and the source through.

2 · A 7-step questionnaire, then "Plan & billing" on the Free plan

After name, type of work, role, tools, problem and goal, "Open workspace" lands on Settings › Plan & billing with the plan still Free, the $1 checkout button below the fold, and a "New workspace" modal on top. The offer says "add a card at sign-up", but in the flow payment is optional and unclear, and the answers given don't change the workspace.
Fix: 2 questions, then checkout, then the Space.

3 · No visible way to invite a teammate

Creating a Space triggers "workspace ready", a calendar pop-up, a "turn a message into a task" pop-up and a 5-step tour, then an empty Space. The sidebar has no Invite button (the landing-page mock shows one). Contacts says "No contact yet". The only invite prompt is a paid banner: "Claim $1.00 and invite up to 4 teammates" (code TEAM1, while checkout uses TEAM-1USD), for teams of 5–25.
Fix: E2's 2-step setup.
