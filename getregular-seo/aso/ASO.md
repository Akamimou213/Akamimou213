# Get Regular: App Store listing plan (ASO + GEO)

**Prepared:** 20 October 2026. **Market:** US English. **Files:** `metadata.json` (copy to paste into App Store Connect), `check_metadata.py` (limits and claim rules; it passes).

## Why the listing is first

The App Store listing does three jobs, and no other page we own does all three:

1. **App Store search.** This is where most installs for a health tracker start.
2. **AI answers.** apps.apple.com was the most-retrieved domain in the 19 Oct proxy panel: **24 of 37** non-branded prompts (`geo/geo-aeo-strategy.md` §0). "Best poop tracker app" answers are assembled from App Store pages and listicles.
3. **Conversion** for every paid click (Meta, TikTok, Apple Ads) and every organic click.

Today the listing works against us on all three:

- The description says "AI-powered stool analysis grounded in clinical standards" and "what may be causing your symptoms". Those are the causal and clinical claims the strategy says to remove (fact sheet §10, C13). AI engines quote this text.
- It never mentions the **Doctor Report**, **manual Bristol entry**, **period tracking** or **iOS only**. Those are the facts that win the "app that makes a PDF for my doctor" and "track stool without photos" prompts, and that stop the "is it on Android" error.
- There are **13 US ratings**, against Bowelle ~2.2K, Plop ~1.5K and Balloon ~1.1K. Rating volume feeds App Store ranking, and AI answers repeat it.

## What Apple actually indexes (sets which field does which job)

| Field | App Store search | AI engines / Google | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Name (30), subtitle (30), keyword field (100 bytes), primary and secondary category | **Indexed** | Read | developer.apple.com/app-store/search (checked 2026-10-20) |
| Description (4,000) | **Not indexed** (strong practitioner consensus; Apple doesn't list it) | **Read and quoted.** This is the GEO field | — |
| Promotional text (170) | "doesn't affect search ranking" (Apple) | Read. Can change without a new build | Apple |
| Custom Product Pages with assigned keywords | Can replace the default page in results for those keywords (since Oct 2025, up to 70 pages) | — | developer.apple.com/news/?id=gf6mgrs6 |
| In-App Events | Searchable; event cards show in results | Indexed on the web | developer.apple.com/app-store/in-app-events |
| App Store Tags (US, en-US) | Generated by Apple from your metadata. You can only deselect them | — | ASC Help, manage-app-tags |

So **the name, subtitle and keywords are written for App Store search, and the description is written for AI engines and humans.** Don't stuff the description with keywords; on iOS it buys nothing.

## Custom Product Pages: one per intent, shared by paid and organic

Each page gets its own first three screenshots, its own promo text, assigned keywords from the keyword field, and a deep link where useful (deep links need iOS 18+ and go through review). Use the same pages as the destination for paid ads with that angle. That makes Meta and TikTok creative, Apple Ads ad variations and organic search one funnel you can measure per page.

| CPP | Assigned keywords (Apple: chosen from the latest approved version's keyword field) | First screenshot message | Paid use | Content cluster / site page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Poop tracker (default page) | none: the default page already ranks on name and subtitle words (poop, tracker, ibs, food, diary) | "Log a poop in 5 seconds: photo or tap" | Broad UA | C1 · `/poop-tracker` |
| IBS | fodmap, symptom | "See what shows up before a flare" | IBS interest and lookalike audiences | C3 · `/ibs-tracker` |
| Bloating | bloating, journal, digestion | "Which meals come before your bloat?" | Bloating hooks | C4 · `/resources/bloating-after-eating` |
| Constipation | constipation, fiber, log | "Track fiber, water and stool type together" | Constipation hooks | C5 · `/resources/constipation-diary` |
| Doctor Report | doctor | "Walk into your GI appointment with a 30-day report" | Retargeting and high-intent "gastroenterologist" audiences | C8 · `/resources/gastroenterologist-appointment-prep` |

Apple recommends a unique keyword set per page, so don't assign one keyword to two CPPs. There's a trade-off: name and subtitle words (poop, ibs) can't be assigned to a CPP unless they're also in the keyword field, and repeating them there wastes bytes. Start with the keyword-field terms above. Move a term only if CPP analytics show it converts better than the default page. Apple doesn't document how many keywords each page can have.

The site's App Store links should point at the matching CPP (`?ppid=`) plus the `pt`/`ct` campaign tokens already in `codex/01` T14. Then App Store Connect shows downloads by page and by site section.

## In-App Events (searchable, and give AI engines fresh dated text)

Run only events backed by something real in the app. Experiments and the Weekly Digest already exist, so these need no new features. Limits: name 30, short description 50, long description 120; up to 10 published at once; each up to 31 days.

| Name (≤30) | Short (≤50) | Badge | Backed by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7-Day Dairy Check | Cut dairy for a week and track how you feel | Challenge | Experiments ("try eliminating dairy for a week" is in the policy) |
| Fiber Week | Add fiber slowly and log stool and bloating | Challenge | Nutrition Score + Experiments |
| Prep for Your GI Visit | Build a 30-day log and a Doctor Report | Special Event | Doctor Report |

Long descriptions are drafted per event at launch, because they must name dates. Re-check claims against `blogs/_EDITORIAL_STANDARD.md` (no outcome promises).

## Ratings: 13 to 150+ without breaking Apple's rules

- **Use only Apple's prompt** (`RequestReviewAction` / `requestReview`). Guideline 5.6.1 disallows custom review prompts. The system shows it at most 3 times per user per 365 days.
- **Ask at value moments:** right after the first Weekly Digest is opened, after a Doctor Report is generated, and on day 14 of a logging streak. Never right after a symptom or bad-day log.
- **No sentiment gating.** Don't show a "Do you like the app?" screen that sends only happy users to the prompt. Apple's guidelines call "filtered" feedback manipulation, and FTC guidance on soliciting reviews warns against review gating. No incentives, and no feature gating on reviews (3.2.2(x)).
- **Reply to every review** in App Store Connect. Replies are public, appear on the web listing and are read by AI engines.
- **Target:** about 150 US ratings within 90 days. That is an assumption, not a forecast: it depends on weekly active loggers, which we don't know. Track it with the App Store lookup API (`userRatingCount`) each week from `geo/tracker`.

## Product Page Optimization tests (one at a time, up to 3 treatments, up to 90 days)

1. **Screenshot 1 message:** "Poop tracker" (control) vs "Doctor Report" vs "Find your trigger foods". Primary metric: conversion rate from product page views.
2. **Icon:** only after test 1, and only with alternate icons included in the binary.
