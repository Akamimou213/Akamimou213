## 2.1 The honesty rule

ZOE's science section works because ZOE runs studies (PREDICT, 60+ papers) and validated its own MenoScale (Kim et al., *Post Reproductive Health* 2025). **Get Regular has none of that yet.** So the section must not imitate it: no "our studies", no "clinically validated", no invented norms. What we can credibly publish:

1. **How our AI works**: inputs, outputs, limits, data flow. This is the most-asked trust question for an AI health app, and ChatGPT fan-out searches brand sites for exactly this.
2. **Method pages for every score**, in plain words.
3. **An evidence library** of every study our content cites (82 on 20 Oct 2026), every reference pulled from PubMed (`science/build_evidence_library.py`).
4. **Topic syntheses**: "what the research says about X", each built on guidelines and trials, with study tables.
5. **Who reviews what**: the clinical advisor's actual role, with consent and disclosure.
6. **A research program page**: the app already has a "Research consent" option (v2.0.0). State what will be studied, how consent works and what will be published. Our first aggregate, de-identified data report becomes the ZOE-style PR and citation asset (STRATEGY §7.6). Don't claim it before it exists.

**Added 20 Oct (verified on NCBI, in `science/extra_pmids.txt`):**
- **Normal ranges:** Mitsuhashi 2018 (NHANES, 95.9% of adults with normal habits go 3–21 times a week), Connell 1965 (origin of "3 and 3"), Blake 2016 (types 3–5 grouped as normal).
- **Transit:** Asnicar 2021 (ZOE-funded blue-dye study; its median figure is in the full text, not the abstract, so the pages don't quote it), Metcalf 1987, Evans 1992.
- **Prevalence:** Palsson 2020 (Rome IV IBS 4.4–4.8%, functional constipation 7.9–8.6%), Almario 2023, Oh 2020.
- **AI evidence:**
  - Stool images: Fagan 2024 and Zhou 2025.
  - Meal photos: Fridolfsson 2025 (GPT-4o/Claude 3.5 energy error, MAPE ~36%) and Chen 2025.
  - The three Dieta stool-AI papers share an author, so check their conflict-of-interest statements before calling them independent.
  - Under Apple Guideline 1.4.1, **don't publish accuracy figures for our own model until it's validated.** Cite these studies as context, not as evidence that Get Regular is accurate.
