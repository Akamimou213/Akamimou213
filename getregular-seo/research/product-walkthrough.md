## 1. The idea in the product's own words

- "Your gut talks. We help you understand it so that you can finally trust it." [AS]
- "Managing symptoms instead of understanding them" is the problem the onboarding opens on. [REC]
- "Turn your gut feelings into gut data." [REC] / "We turn gut feelings into gut data." [SITE]
- "Every entry adds to your evolving gut profile." [AS]
- "It's time to trust your gut. Now you have the data to back it up." [AS]

**What it is, plainly:** a daily log of stool, meals, drinks and how you feel. The app scores it, finds patterns over days and weeks, suggests one change to test, and gives you a report for your doctor. It goes past digestion: skin, hair, energy, mood and nutrient gaps are first-class goals.

## 4. Logging

**Stool ("Log Stool")** [AS][REC]
- **Bristol Stool Type**, types 1–7 with in-app icons and labels: Pebbles; Lumpy, firm; Sausage-like, cracks; Sausage-like, smooth; Soft blobs; Fluffy pieces; Watery.
- **Color:** Medium/Dark brown, Light brown, Green, Yellow, Pale, Black, Red.
- **Surface texture:** Smooth, Minor cracks, Mucus or oily.
- **Buoyancy:** Fully floating, Somewhat floating, Sinks. **Water clarity:** Clear water, Some cloudiness, Very cloudy, Disintegrating.
- Time on the toilet (slider), notes, photo or manual. Also: "Logging a stool without details will not generate a score and analysis."

**Meal ("Log Meal")** [REC]
- Photo or "Describe your meal" text, with "Tips for better tracking" (portion sizes, cooking method, ingredients, sauces). "Had at" time, meal type, notes.
- **"I'm considering":** score a food *before* you eat it (the homepage's "Check your food score first").

## 5. What the app gives back

**Gut Health detail** (per stool) [REC]
- A score with a label (e.g. 85 "Optimal"), a plain-language summary, and **Score Contributors**:
  - Form (e.g. 75% "Formed but not hard");
  - Color (75% "Light brown · slightly outside the ideal range");
  - Texture (100% "Smooth · healthy hydration status");
  - Buoyancy, Residue.
- A footer: "informational purposes only… not intended to replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment."

**Nutrition detail** (per meal) [AS][REC]
- A score and label (e.g. 81 "Good Nutrition"), a summary, tags (High-Protein, Prebiotic-Rich, Carb-Heavy), ingredients with portions, and **"How to Improve This Meal"**.
- Score Contributors: **Digestive support, Inflammation impact, Microbiome fuel, Food integrity** (percentages, each with a reason).
- Nutrition breakdown: macros, sugar, **lactose, fructose**, micronutrients, **omega-3 (DHA)**. "A full nutrition breakdown… *without* the calorie tracking."

**The Digest** ("Insights, gentle shifts, and what's been moving") [AS][REC]
- **Weekly Digest:** needs at least 4 days of logging in a week. Cards are tagged by type, for example:
  - **NUTRIENT:** "Your fiber intake averaged 5g per day this week, which is significantly below the recommended 25–30g."
  - **NUTRIENT:** magnesium "30mg per day, only 7.5% of the 400mg target."
  - **GOAL PROGRESS:** "Your meals with salmon, rich in omega-3s, have been linked to positive symptom days."
- **Daily Digest** on the trend screen: "Great day overall! Your gut health and nutrition scores are both strong…"
- **Gut Check** (premium): "Trust your gut, guided by your data." Prompt chips:
  - What caused my symptoms today?
  - What should I eat to feel better tomorrow?
  - Analyze my last 7 days
  - Any patterns you notice?
  - Suggest supplements for what I logged
  - What should I try next?

  "Conversations stored to your core memory for better insights."
- **Try This** (experiments, premium): "Guided, science-backed experiments to help you track what's working and what isn't." Each experiment has a goal, steps and 7 tap-to-complete days, and ends with an outcome:
  - *Add kiwi to your mornings*: "Two green kiwis a day may ease bloating and improve regularity." Eat 2 green kiwis each morning, log bowel movements and bloating, and the app compares before and after.
  - *Hair Health Nutrient Boost* (B12, magnesium, potassium foods).
  - *Gentle Recovery Diet*: "Felt better · More regular".
  - *Dairy Elimination*: "No change", with the note *"Nothing happened, I think I can have cheese."* An experiment can **clear** a food, not just blame one.
- **Recipes** (premium): "Daily recipes personalized for you", e.g. Ginger-Miso Salmon Bowl, tagged Anti-Inflammatory, with protein and carbs.

**Backlog** [REC]: a month calendar heatmap of the three scores, with a symptom highlight filter.

**Reports for your Doctor** [REC]: "Generate a report summarizing your gut health, nutrition, symptoms, and supplements over a selected period." Choose last 14, 30, 60 or 90 days, or custom; pick up to 10 symptoms; optionally include goals and dietary preferences.

## 9. New issues to send to the team

1. **Stool type labels** in the app ("Sausage-like, cracks / smooth", "Soft blobs", "Fluffy pieces") closely follow the licensed Bristol Stool Form Scale descriptors. Add this to counsel's Bristol licensing question (`tools/README.md`).
2. **"Built with board-certified gastroenterologist*s*"** (App Store screenshot, plural) vs. one named advisor on the site. Make them consistent.
3. **"Science-backed experiments"** and "uncover the root cause" (Today card): stronger than the evidence for every experiment. Prefer "guided experiments" and "find patterns". The kiwi experiment does have trial evidence; others may not.
4. **"Suggest supplements for what I logged"** (Gut Check chip): supplement advice is a sensitive claim. Check that the answer carries limits.
5. Health labels such as **"Optimal"** on a single stool: fine as wellness language, but keep "not a diagnosis" visible.
6. **Onboarding asks ethnicity, sex and birth year** alongside symptoms. All of that is consumer health data under WA MHMDA. Make sure the consent text covers it.
