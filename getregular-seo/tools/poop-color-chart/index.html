    How to tell if it's food

    Look back a day.Then watch and wait.

    Think yesterday

    Food usually takes a day or more to pass, so think about yesterday's meals: beets, berries, greens, dyed drinks.

    Check how you feel

    If you feel fine and don't have diarrhea, a new color is most likely from something you ate.

    Don't wait on these

    Black and tarry, maroon, visible blood, or pale stool with yellow skin: call a doctor instead of waiting.

    What color should poop be?

    The adult color chart, what each color can mean, and when not to wait.

    Short answer

    Brown, in any shade from light to dark. Green, yellow, orange or reddish poop is usually from food or dye and goes back to brown within a few days. Black and tarry, red or maroon, and pale or clay-colored stool can signal bleeding or a bile, liver or pancreas problem, and need a doctor [1].

    Which medicines change poop color?

    Iron supplements, bismuth (Pepto-Bismol) and activated charcoal can turn stool black without any bleeding [2]. Antibiotics can turn it greenish [3]. Anti-diarrheal medicine and barium from an X-ray test can make it pale. Tell your doctor about these anyway; they can also hide a problem.

    Questions

    What color poop is bad?

    Black and tarry, red or maroon (possible blood), and pale, white or clay-colored stool need the most attention [1][2].

    Why is my poop black after Pepto-Bismol or iron?

    Bismuth and iron commonly turn stool black without bleeding [2]. Stool that's sticky, tarry and smells unusually strong is different: get it checked the same day.

    How long does red poop last after beets?

    Usually until the beets have passed, often a day or two. If red continues after that, or you didn't eat red foods, contact a doctor.

    Is this for babies?

    No. Baby and infant stool colors follow different rules; ask your pediatrician.
