      What does my poop type mean?

      The 7 types, what's normal, how accurate the chart is, and when to see a doctor.

      Short answer

      Stool shape is a rough guide to how fast food moves through your gut. Hard pellets (types 1–2) usually mean slow transit, smooth soft logs (types 3–4) are often considered ideal, and mushy or liquid stool (types 6–7) means fast transit [1]. Most people vary from day to day, so a week of logs says more than one bowel movement [3].

      The 7 stool types at a glance

      TypeWhat it looks likeWhat it usually suggests

      Is the stool chart accurate?

      Reasonably. In the original study, stool type tracked whole-gut transit time measured with markers [1]. When people classified stool models, 81% were correct overall, but borderline types (2, 3, 5 and 6) were harder [2]. More in what the research says about the stool chart.

      What's normal?

      In a US national survey, 96% of adults with normal bowel habits went between three times a day and three times a week [4]. Shape varies too: in a UK study only about 56–61% of stools were the "normal" types [3]. A lasting change from your normal matters more than any single day.

      When to see a doctor

      • Blood in your stool, or black, tarry stool
      • Diarrhea lasting more than 2 days, high fever, 6 or more loose stools a day, or signs of dehydration [5]
      • Constipation with constant belly pain, vomiting, fever, not being able to pass gas, or weight loss without trying [6]
      • A change in bowel habits that lasts, especially if you're 50 or older or have a family history of bowel cancer

      Questions

      What's the healthiest poop type?

      Types 3 and 4 are often considered ideal: formed, soft and easy to pass. Type 5 is common too, and some researchers group types 3–5 as normal [2].

      Is it bad to have type 6 or 7 sometimes?

      An occasional loose stool after coffee, a big meal or a stomach bug is common. Diarrhea lasting more than 2 days, or with fever, blood or dehydration, needs a doctor [5].

      Does my week log get saved anywhere?

      Only in your own browser. It isn't sent to us. Clear it any time.

      Can an app tell my type from a photo?

      Some studies have tested AI on stool photos, with promising but early results. See what the research says and how our AI works.
