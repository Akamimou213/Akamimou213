How do I prepare for a gastroenterologist appointment?

Short answer: write down your top three questions, most important first [1], bring a list of every medicine and supplement, and bring 2–4 weeks of symptom and bowel logs. Mention any warning signs (blood, black stool, weight loss, night symptoms) at the start. Leave knowing which symptoms should make you call back sooner.

Why logs beat memory

When researchers compared recall questionnaires with prospective diaries in people with GI symptoms, recall agreed poorly with what people had recorded day by day [2]. See what the research says about diaries vs memory.

Questions about tests

TestWorth asking
Blood tests (blood count, CRP, celiac antibodies)Do I need to fast? Should I keep eating gluten until the celiac test? [3]
Stool tests (calprotectin, infection, FIT)How do I collect and return the sample? What does a borderline result mean?
Colonoscopy or upper endoscopyWhy do I need it? What prep will I use? Will you take biopsies? Do I need a ride home?
Breath testsWhat should I eat beforehand, and which medicines should I pause?

Frequently asked questions

Is my sheet saved anywhere?

No. It's built in your browser and isn't sent to us unless you choose to email it to yourself.

What if I think I have IBS?

Ask which criteria you meet, which subtype, and which tests ruled out other conditions. The ACG guideline supports a positive, symptom-based diagnosis with a few targeted tests [4].
