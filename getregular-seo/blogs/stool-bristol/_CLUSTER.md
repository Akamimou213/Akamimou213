## Cross-cutting reviewer and editor notes

- Several posts cite the same core sources: Lewis & Heaton 1997, Cleveland Clinic pages, NIDDK, NHS bowel cancer symptoms, and MedlinePlus. Every URL was opened on 19 Oct 2026, except the Mayo Clinic FAQ (seen in search results only) and Levitt & Duane 1972 (PubMed record, no abstract).
- The red-flag lists use "new symptoms after age 50", per the editorial standard. USPSTF screening starts at 45 (cited in the mucus post). The reviewer should choose one age to use consistently.
- Image placeholders: every post has one inline `IMAGE BRIEF` placeholder plus an `og_image_brief`; all specify illustrations, not realistic stool photos.
