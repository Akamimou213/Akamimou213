The new reality for app founders

Shipping an app got easy.
Getting paying users didn't.

Anyone can ship now. That means more apps fighting for the same feeds, the same keywords and the same users, while the ad platforms grade their own homework. Most founders we talk to aren't confused. They're stretched: CEO, product manager and the whole growth team at once.

01

“I'm testing ads at midnight after building all day.”

You know creative testing matters. But making 8–15 new ads a month (hooks, scripts, versions) is a full-time job. You already have one.

02

“My install cost looks fine. Are we making money?”

Without proper tracking you're scaling on numbers that flatter the platform, not numbers that tell the truth about who pays.

03

“People install… then vanish before paying.”

The ad worked. Then the store page, onboarding or paywall lost them. Nobody owns the space between the click and the payment, so it leaks.

04

“Agencies sell me installs. I need paying users.”

Cheap installs that never pay are the most expensive thing you can buy. Most agencies stop at the install, because that's where their report ends.

Straight answers

Frequently asked questions

Why only $1,500 when agencies charge $5K+?

Because you work directly with the person running your ads, creative, ASO and SEO, not a junior wrapped in agency overhead. No account managers, no markup. It's a founding rate for the first 5 app clients, locked for 12 months. After that, it goes up.

What ad budget do I need?

Around $2,000 a month in ad spend is a workable minimum: enough to test ads properly and learn fast. Below that, take the free Growth Plan and come back when you're ready. Most clients spend $3K–$50K a month.

We already run ads ourselves. What would you add?

Usually three things: more tested ads (8–15 a month vs your 1–2), tracking that tells the truth (self-run accounts often teach the platforms from weak signals, so the algorithm finds the wrong users), and the funnel after the click: store page, quiz, paywall. Your free Growth Plan shows which one costs you most. If none do, we'll say so.

Our tracking is a mess, or doesn't exist. Is that a problem?

It's normal, and it's the first thing we fix, in week 1. We set up the events that matter (install, signup, trial, payment) so ad platforms learn from real buyers and your dashboard tells the truth. No jargon, no 40-page audit. Just working numbers.

Do you do AI / ChatGPT visibility?

Yes, as part of organic growth. Each month we check whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews mention your app for the questions your users ask, then improve the sources those answers draw on: your site, store listing, reviews, comparison pages and communities. Good ASO and SEO feed this too. Nobody controls these models, so we report what moved and never promise a placement.

Which apps are a good fit?

Consumer apps on iOS and Android, especially subscription and in-app purchase apps, with roughly $2K+ a month to spend on ads and a product people already keep using. If retention is the real problem, the Growth Plan will say that before you spend more on ads.

What do you need from me?

Three things: access (ad accounts, store console, analytics), feedback on ads and tests within 48 hours, and one 30-minute call a week. Founders typically spend under 2 hours a week on this.

Am I locked into a contract?

No lock-in. One 90-day sprint to start (the honest minimum for ad testing and funnel fixes to show up in your numbers), then month-to-month. Cancel anytime after.
