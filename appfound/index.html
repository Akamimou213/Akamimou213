    The new reality for app founders

    Shipping an app got easy.
    Getting paying users didn't.

    Anyone can ship an app now, so you're competing with more apps for the same feeds, keywords and users. Ad platforms report their own results. Most founders we talk to know what to do. They don't have the hours: they're the CEO, the PM and the whole growth team.

    01 · Time

    “I'm testing ads at midnight after building all day.”

    Creative is the biggest lever you have, but producing 8–15 new ads a month is a full-time job. You already have one.

    02 · Trust

    “My install cost looks fine. Are we making money?”

    If the platforms can't see trials and purchases, they optimize for cheap taps. You scale numbers that flatter them, not you.

    03 · Leaks

    “People install, then vanish before paying.”

    The ad did its job. Then the store page, onboarding or paywall lost them. Nobody owns the gap between click and payment.

    04 · Incentives

    “Agencies sell me installs. I need subscribers.”

    Installs that never pay are the most expensive thing you can buy. Most agency reports stop at the install. Ours start there.

    Frequently asked questions

    Something else? Ask Mohamed on LinkedIn ↗

    What's in the free Growth Plan, and what's the catch?

    A short video and a written plan: your funnel from ad to payment, your biggest leak, the first three fixes ranked by impact, five creative angles from your reviews, and a tracking check. No catch. It's how we show our thinking before you spend a dollar. If we're not the right fit, we'll say so in the plan.

    What ad budget do I need?

    Around $2,000 a month in ad spend is a workable minimum: enough to test ads properly and learn fast. Below that, take the free plan and come back when you're ready. Most clients spend $3K–$50K a month.

    How fast will I see results?

    Tracking and funnel fixes usually show in weeks 1–3. Creative testing needs 3–6 weeks of spend to find reliable winners. Organic channels compound over months. That's why we start with a 90-day sprint and agree on targets in week 1.

    We already run ads ourselves. What would you add?

    Usually three things: more tested ads (8–15 a month instead of 1–2), tracking that tells the truth (self-run accounts often teach the platforms from weak signals, so they find the wrong users), and the funnel after the click: store page, onboarding, paywall. Your free plan shows which one costs you most.

    How do you measure results after Apple's privacy changes (ATT, SKAdNetwork)?

    Two layers, always labelled. Platform numbers (SKAdNetwork and modeled conversions) guide day-to-day optimization. Your own source of truth (subscription platform, MMP or product analytics) decides budgets. If the two disagree, your data wins.

    Do I need an MMP like AppsFlyer or Adjust?

    Not to start. At smaller budgets, platform SDKs plus your subscription platform and product analytics usually cover it. We'll tell you when an MMP starts paying for itself, and set it up when it does.

    Our tracking is a mess, or doesn't exist. Is that a problem?

    It's normal, and it's the first thing we fix, in week 1. We set up the events that matter (install, signup, trial, payment) so the platforms learn from real buyers and your dashboard tells the truth. Usually one ticket for your developer, or none.

    Do you do AI / ChatGPT visibility?

    Yes, as part of organic growth. Each month we check whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews mention your app for the questions your users ask, then improve the sources those answers draw on. Good ASO and SEO feed this too. Nobody controls these models, so we report what moved and never promise a placement.

    Who owns the ad accounts, creatives and data?

    You do. We work inside your accounts with the access you grant, and every creative, dashboard and learnings doc stays with you if you leave.

    Which apps are a good fit?

    Consumer apps on iOS and Android, especially subscription and in-app purchase apps, with roughly $2K+ a month for ads and a product people already come back to. If retention is the real problem, the Growth Plan will say so before you spend more on ads.

    What do you need from me?

    Access (ad accounts, store console, analytics, billing), feedback on ads and tests within 48 hours, and one 30-minute call a week. Founders typically spend under 2 hours a week on this.

    Am I locked into a contract?

    No. One 90-day sprint to start (the honest minimum for testing and funnel fixes to show up in your numbers), then month-to-month. Cancel anytime after.
