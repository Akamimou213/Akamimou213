# Only the owner can do these: <client>

In order. Each one says why and how long it takes.

1. **Google Search Console**: verify the domain property, submit the sitemap, add us as a restricted (read-only) user. ~10 min.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools**: sign in, import from Search Console, submit the sitemap, turn on IndexNow. Its AI Performance report shows when Copilot cites the site. ~10 min.
3. **"How did you hear about us?"** on the lead/estimate form, with AI options (ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude) in the middle of the list. We draft it; the owner approves it.
4. **Confirm the fact sheet** (`facts.md`): every price, area, hour and warranty line.
5. **Business Profile, Apple Business, Bing Places** (local businesses): check name, category, hours, holiday hours and service areas against the fact sheet.
6. **Directories and trade listings**: 20 to 30, the same name, one-liner and logo everywhere.
7. **Reviews**: one ask-every-customer routine with the platform's own link. No gating, no incentives.
8. **Videos**: one per decision page where Google shows videos for the query.
