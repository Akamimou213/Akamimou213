# LinkedIn content strategy: Meta ads, performance marketing, AI

Goal: get D2C/Shopify founders and app operators to DM or book a call, so inbound replaces the Upwork bidding.
Cadence: 2 posts a day, 7 days a week for the first 30 days, then review.
Research date: 19 Nov 2026 (Context news + LinkedIn search, Firecrawl on X).

---

## 1. What the research says

**What's already saturated on LinkedIn (last 30 days):**
- "Andromeda 2.0" explainers. After Meta's 28 Oct post (1B+ ads scored per second, +15.7% conversions on Facebook in Q2), dozens of marketers posted the same summary.
- "Test concepts, not variations." Everyone says it. Almost no one shows an ad-level screenshot that proves it.
- "Turn Claude into your marketing team / 40 AI agents" listicles. High reach, low trust, and no client results.

**What almost nobody is posting (your gap):**
1. Real account numbers with the method written next to them (platform vs. backend). You already have this on your site; most posters have none.
2. Tracking. Everyone talks creative; few talk about the pixel counting wrong, which is your lever 02.
3. Founder-language posts. 90% of Meta-ads posts are written for other media buyers. Your buyers are founders. Write for the person who pays.
4. Apps + e-com in one voice. You did 116,464 first deliveries at $1.81 for a super app. That is a rare story on a feed full of Shopify gurus.
5. Practical AI with a guardrail: "here's the agent I use, here's what I don't let it touch."

**Timely hooks to use this week and next:**
- **Meta's new Commercial/Self-Serve Ad Terms take effect 20 Nov 2026.** Advertisers are "solely responsible" for anything Meta's AI enhancements produce, and Meta gets permission to crawl your site. Brands are already pulling ads with unwanted AI "glow-ups" (Adweek via Yahoo, 22 Oct).
- **Meta price per ad up ~12–14% YoY**, Northbeam says CPMs up ~25%; some buyers are moving budget to "Q5" (late Dec–Jan) (Marketing Brew, 8 Oct).
- **Black Friday is 27 Nov.** Triple Whale data: 33,000+ shops, Meta took 67.6% of spend at 2.26 ROAS, CPM +7.8% to $22.26 last BFCM.
- **Meta AI Business Assistant** (Q3 call): adopters got ~5% more conversions at the same cost. A maple syrup brand got +57% purchases from *longer* copy.

## 5. Formats that work right now

1. **Text + one screenshot** (redacted Ads Manager / Shopify). Best for Proof.
2. **Carousel / PDF** (6–8 slides). Best for Leaks and frameworks. Saves = reach.
3. **Plain text, short lines.** Best for opinions and stories.
4. **30–60s vertical video** of you talking or screen-recording an account audit. You already have the reel; reuse cuts of it.
5. **Poll** once a week max, only with a follow-up post on the results.

Post skeleton (use it until it's automatic):
```
Hook (one line, a number or a claim, under 12 words)
Second line that makes them click "see more"

The context (2–3 lines)
What I did / what to check (3–5 short steps or bullets)
The result + how it was measured

One-line takeaway
Soft CTA: "If your account does X, send me 'audit' and I'll check it."
```
No links in the body (LinkedIn cuts reach). Put the Calendly link in the first comment.

## 6. X → LinkedIn repurpose workflow (daily, 20 minutes)

1. **Find (5 min).** Ask Claude: "Firecrawl search X for Meta ads / ad creative / AI media buying posts from the last 7 days, sorted by engagement." Accounts worth watching (seen in this research): @adamtaylorl (ad angles), @sourfraser (BFCM data), @shauneng (funnel structure), @FedotOff90 (static ad types), @peterczepiga (scaling rules).
2. **Pick one idea, not the post.** Keep the insight, drop their words.
3. **Add your touch (the part that matters):**
   - one number or screenshot from your own accounts, or
   - a disagreement ("he's right about X, but in apps it breaks because Y"), or
   - a founder translation ("what this means for a $30K/month Shopify store").
4. **Rewrite in the skeleton above.** Credit the person if the idea is clearly theirs ("Adam Taylor posted a framework I've been using…"); this also earns you their audience's attention.
5. **Never** copy a thread line by line. LinkedIn readers also read X, and it kills trust.

Example, done for you, from Adam Taylor's 14 Nov post ("the first sentence of your ad decides who Meta shows it to") → see Post 3 below.

## 8. Four ready-to-post drafts

### Post 1 — Meta news (Day 1, Post A)

```
Meta's new ad terms start today.

Most brands haven't read them. Here's the part that matters:

If Meta's AI changes your ad (adds a translation, rebuilds your image,
turns your photos into a carousel), YOU are responsible for what it says.
Not Meta.

Agencies are already pulling live ads with AI "glow-ups"
nobody approved.

3 things I'm checking in every account this week:

1. Ad level → Advantage+ creative → turn off every enhancement
   you haven't tested on purpose. Check "advanced preview" too;
   some only switch off there.
2. Look at the ads that are live now, not just new ones.
   New enhancements get added to existing ads.
3. If you're in a regulated niche (health, supplements, finance),
   turn them all off until you've reviewed them.

AI enhancements can work. Test them like any other change:
one at a time, against a control.

Not sure what's switched on in your account?
Comment "terms" and I'll tell you where to look.
```

### Post 3 — X repurpose (Day 2, Post B)

```
The first line of your ad decides who Meta shows it to.

Since Andromeda, Meta reads the ad itself to find the audience.
So your hook isn't just copy. It's your targeting.

Adam Taylor posted a great breakdown of this. Here it is
for a Shopify brand selling, say, a posture corrector:

Unaware: "I didn't know my neck pain started at my desk."
Problem aware: "If your shoulders ache by 3pm, read this."
Solution aware: "Stretching every day and still sore? It's not the muscles."
Product aware: "Before you buy another posture corrector, watch this."
Most aware: "Think it's too expensive? 30 seconds."

Most accounts I audit only run the last two.
That's why CPA climbs the moment they scale:
Meta keeps showing ads to the same small group who already knows them.

You don't need all five.
Move your best angle one level up and you're talking to a bigger pool.

Which level does your best ad sit at?
```

### Post 4 — Proof (Day 3, Post A) + screenshot `public/proof/d2c-wellness-advantage.webp`

$11,462 spend. $114,150 purchase value. 9.96x ROAS.

Before you screenshot this for your boss, the honest part:

That's platform-attributed ROAS. Meta counting its own homework.
Here's what I did so the number was worth trusting:

## 9. Turning posts into calls

- Reply to every comment within the first hour (that's what drives reach).
- Anyone who comments a keyword ("audit", "terms") → DM within 24h with one specific question about their account, not a pitch.
- Every day: comment thoughtfully on 10 posts by founders/D2C operators (not other media buyers). Use your target clients as the list.
- Once a week, a direct CTA post: "3 free audits this month, reply 'audit'."
- Keep sending Upwork proposals, but only to clients with hire history and spend > $1K.
