# Same Lunch, Different Day — voice-over script for ElevenLabs

**Voice:** a warm, conversational woman, late 20s to 30s, US accent. Calm and curious, like a friend explaining
something she just figured out. Not an announcer, not salesy.

**Settings (Eleven Multilingual v2):** Stability 45–55 % · Similarity 75 % · Style 0–15 % · Speaker boost on · Speed 0.95.
**Pronunciation:** Monash = "MON-ash". Get Regular = two clear words.

**Export:** one file per line, MP3, named exactly as the ID below (e.g. `same.mp3`). No music, no added silence;
the pauses between lines are set in the edit.

| # | ID | Line | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `same` | Same lunch. Fine on Monday. Rough on Thursday. | Three short beats, a small pause after each. |
| 2 | `lunch` | So is it the lunch? | A real question, light. |
| 3 | `cup` | Gut researchers at Monash University describe it more like a cup than a switch. | Lean on "cup" and "switch". |
| 4 | `stack` | Each food adds a little. Eat a few close together, and they stack up. | Matter-of-fact. |
| 5 | `other` | And other things can add to it too. | Slight lift, "there's more". |
| 6 | `three` | A short night. A stressful week. The days around your period. | A list, a beat between items. |
| 7 | `spill` | So on Thursday, the same lunch is the drop that spills it. | Build slightly to "spills it". |
| 8 | `never` | It was never just the lunch. It was everything in the cup. | Softer, the insight. Lean on "everything". |
| 9 | `app` | Get Regular lets you log your meals, symptoms, bathroom trips and mood, day by day, so you can see what fills yours. | Warm, unhurried; "yours" lands. |
| 10 | `close` | Notice what fills your cup. | Gentle close. |

111 words; aim for about 45–50 s of speech in total (about 2.3–2.6 words per second).
