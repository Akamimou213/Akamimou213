Status: **draft, waiting for the story check.** Nothing is animated yet.

## The series

**Name (working): "How your gut keeps time."** Top of funnel, for people who are problem-unaware or solution-unaware.
Each film teaches one true pattern about the gut. Get Regular appears only at the end, as a way to *see your own pattern*,
never as a cure. Every film ends on the same idea: **you can't see a pattern in a day; you can in a few weeks.**

## Film 1 — The Wrong Suspect

- **Length / format:** ~50 s, 9:16 (1080×1920), Stories/Reels/TikTok first.
- **Logline:** Everyone blames their last meal; the gut runs about a day behind, so the real reason is usually already further
  down the line, and only a few weeks of notes can show it.
- **Device you can say in a sentence:** every meal is a paper carriage on one slow train through the body; the carriage at
  the station gets blamed for what an earlier carriage did.
- **Colour means one thing:** Get Regular gold = the true pattern (the line that connects the days). Never decoration.
- **Story format:** a token travelling through stations (the train through the gut), voice-led clock: each picture change lands
  ~0.3 s after a sentence ends.
- **Character:** one original paper character (the viewer's stand-in), not a brand mascot or a patient. No body-shaming
  framing; the belly is never the punchline.

### Rules this script follows
- Never asserts the viewer has a condition (Meta personal-attributes policy). "Most of us" and "your gut" mean the body in general.
- No cure, fix, heal, detox, debloat or flat-belly language. Hedged: "may", "often", "about".
- No stool photos or realistic stool; abstract icons only.
- Brand in the last ~12 s (≈ 20 %).
