# 2 — Ideation: persona, angle, pain point, motion theme

Options for each choice, then the pick and why. The pick is a recommendation; swap any row and the brief follows.

## Persona

| | Persona | Who | Reachable on Meta? | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | **Rental manager / growing plex owner** (gestion locative) | Owner-operators and small-to-mid rental firms, Québec; the person who runs the 1st of the month (owner, office manager, bookkeeper). Often on Proprio Expert or Plexflow, CORPIQ members | **Yes**: large population of plex owners and rental managers, interest and lookalike targeting work | Medium per account, high volume |
| P2 | Condo-management firm (gestion de copropriétés) | Firm owner / controller; growing number of syndicates, Loi 16 pressure | Narrow (hundreds of firms): better for LinkedIn / ABM | High per account |
| P3 | Self-managed syndicate (board treasurer) | Volunteer treasurer signing cheques | Yes, but low value and long cycle | Low |

**Pick: P1, the rental manager.** Meta's strength is reach into a broad, recognisable crowd; P1 is the biggest of the
three and lives the clearest monthly pain. P2 gets its own cut later (angle I3 below) or a LinkedIn test.

## Angle

| | Angle | Insight | Hook line (verbatim) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A1** | **Le 1er du mois** — the monthly crisis becomes an ordinary day | I1 | « Le 1er du mois mobilise toute votre équipe » → « c'est juste une journée comme toutes les autres » |
| A2 | Exceptions only — automation as more control | I4 | « Ne gérez plus chaque paiement, gérez seulement les exceptions. » |
| A3 | The bridge — your systems finally talk | I2 | « Vos systèmes ne communiquent pas. Vos équipes font le raccord manuellement. » |
| A4 | More doors, not more processes | I3 (P2) | « Vous ajoutez des portes, pas des processus. » |

**Pick: A1.** It's a date, so it's instantly understood with the sound off, it's specific to this persona, and it's
timeable: the ad runs in the last week of each month, when the dread is real. TOMSO already owns the line. A2 and A3
become the hook variants for the test (same body, different first 3 s).

## Pain point

**Pick: the first days of the month swallowed by collection work.** In TOMSO's words: « Chèques à traiter, portails bancaires
à consulter, paiements à vérifier et NSF à suivre ». Four concrete objects, all visual, each one a beat. Secondary pain we
resolve on screen: NSF found « d'un coup d'œil » instead of logging into each bank (Brittany: « nous devions nous connecter
pour voir les paiements retournés »).

## Motion theme

| | Theme | What moves | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M1** | **« Le 1er »: a month calendar drawn in TOMSO's line** | The 1st's cell swells and fills with cheques, portal tabs and NSF flags until it crowds the whole month; TOMSO's line threads through it (logiciel ○—○ banques), the clutter resolves, and the 1st shrinks back to the same size as every other day | Turns the angle into one picture: « une journée comme toutes les autres » = a cell the same size as the others |
| M2 | « Le fil »: tangle → straight line → spiral | The brand's line motif from static 1 | Strong brand fit, but abstract: it doesn't say « 1er du mois » by itself |
| M3 | Kinetic type only | TOMSO's caps headlines in Faculty Glyphic | Cheap and clear, weakest stop power |

**Pick: M1, with M2 inside it.** The calendar carries the meaning; the brand's line with ring nodes is how TOMSO acts on
it (it untangles the 1st's cell). Everything drawn in the statics' language: 2 px ink lines, open ring nodes, flat grounds,
lime once per frame. No invented product screens.

## Voice-over?

Meta autoplays muted, so the film works with text alone (version **T**). We also test a voiced version (**V**): a calm
Québec-French voice reading the same verbatim lines, with burned-in captions. Same picture, so the test isolates the voice.
