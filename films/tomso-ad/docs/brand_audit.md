# TOMSO — brand and website audit

Sources: all 34 pages and posts in tomso.ca's sitemaps (17 FR + 17 EN), crawled 2026-10-30 at the site's 3 s crawl delay;
the site's CSS; `capture.cjs` screenshots; five static ads supplied by the client (`refs/statics/1–5`). Copy below is quoted
verbatim (French, as published). Nothing here is invented; gaps are marked **unknown**.

## 1. What TOMSO sells

A payments layer between property-management software and the client's banks. In their words: « TOMSO fait le lien entre
votre logiciel de gestion immobilière et vos comptes bancaires. » It does not replace the management software (« Non. TOMSO
travaille avec votre logiciel de gestion actuel. ») and the client keeps their banks (« Dois-je changer de banque…? Non. »).

What it does (FAQ, solution pages):
- **Collecting** rent and condo fees by pre-authorised debit (« prélèvements »), sent from the management software or TOMSO's
  web interface, processed on the scheduled date; funds « généralement déposés … dès le jour ouvrable suivant »
  (marketed as « Dépôts en moins de 24 h »), straight into the owner's or client's account, « sans compte intermédiaire ».
- **Exceptions**: rejected debits / NSF flagged with tenant, building and amount; reports by email (« dans les 24 h à 48 h »,
  Proprio Expert post).
- **Paying suppliers** without cheques: prepare → approve (custom approval circuits, the right signatories per entity) →
  execute, with a full audit trail; invoices attachable.
- **Many accounts, one process**: several banks, entities, buildings, syndicates in one place; condo managers can start
  debits « sans être signataire au compte ».
- **Cheque scanning** (condo page): one scanner deposits into any bank.
- Setup: account « généralement … dans les 72 heures » after documents; assisted deployment; pricing on request (services,
  number of accounts, volume).
- Integrations (partners page): Condo Manager, Proprio Expert, Plexflow, Hopem, Regisco, Appnflat. Associations: RGCQ, AQGC,
  CORPIQ.
- Compliance: « Enregistrement CANAFE et Banque du Canada » (home); « permis en règle délivré par l'Autorité des marchés
  financiers » (FAQ).

## 2. Who buys it

Stated (FAQ « À qui s'adresse TOMSO? »): property managers, owners who self-manage, self-managed condo syndicates, condo
management firms. Two product doors, two pains:

La crise, non. » |
| **Condo management (copropriété)** | /solution-pour-coproprietes | « Chaque nouveau syndicat ajoute des coûts » — new bank account, signatories, approvals | « La gestion des paiements gruge vos marges. Mettez-y fin. » « Vous ajoutez des portes, pas des processus. » |

The real buyer is the firm owner / controller / accounting manager of a growing portfolio (case study: Eastern Ontario
Property Management, 500 → 4 000 doors « sans augmenter son personnel »). Growth is the trigger: « Plus le portefeuille grandit,
plus ça se multiplie. » Market: Canada; HQ Montréal (Place Ville-Marie); French first, English second; clients in Québec and
Ontario. Brand is new: « Otonom Solution devient TOMSO » (22 Sept 2026) — so ads also do name recognition.

## 3. Message architecture (as the site states it)

- **Promise (H1):** « La fin de la complexité financière en gestion immobilière »
- **Tagline:** « Encaissez plus vite. Payez simplement. »
- **Mechanism:** « TOMSO fait circuler l'information et les paiements entre vos logiciels de gestion et vos banques. »
- **Enemy:** manual bridging — « Vos systèmes ne communiquent pas. Vos équipes font le raccord manuellement. »
- **Pillars:** encaisser plus vite · garder le contrôle · libérer l'équipe des tâches répétitives
- **Belief (about page):** « on ne l'optimise pas. On l'élimine. »
- **Close:** « La complexité financière s'arrête ici. » CTA « Planifier une démo » (30-minute demo with an expert).

Voice: short declaratives in pairs and triplets, often a two-beat turn (« Vos logiciels. Vos banques. Enfin connectés. »,
« Moins de tâches. Plus de contrôle. », « Gérez plus. Contrôlez mieux. »). Calm, confident, no hype, no exclamation marks;
vouvoiement; Québec business French (« gruge », « NSF », « portes »). The ads keep that rhythm.

## 5. Visual identity

**Colour** (sampled from the statics; hex values match the site CSS where both exist):

| Role | Hex | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| Accent / CTA (the one constant) | `#E4FF7A` lime | every static's CTA pill, static 2's second headline line, site buttons |
| Ink | `#000000` on light (statics) · `#262423` (site text) · `#1F1E1C` | headlines, line art |
| Mint ground | `#B6D4C8` | static 1 |
| Slate ground | `#3C5B72` | static 2 (white + lime type), static 5 CTA |
| Lavender ground | `#C9D4E8` | statics 3, 5; static 4 CTA |
| Paper ground | `#F6F5F1` (site `#F6F5F2`) | static 4, site sections |
| Site extras | `#EAF6C3` pale lime (hero ground), `#6296B8`, `#CECEEB` | website only |

One flat ground per frame, no gradients, lime used once per frame (the CTA, or one highlighted line).

**Type** (from the site CSS, OFL-licensed Google Fonts, safe for ads):
- **Faculty Glyphic** for headlines: the statics set it in ALL CAPS, tight leading; its Q with the detached tail stroke is
  a signature detail (ÉQUIPE, CHAQUE, UNIQUEMENT, QUE). The site uses sentence case.
- **Geist** for body copy (sentence case, ink or navy).
- **Geist Mono** for CTAs and labels, sentence case in the statics, uppercase on the site.
- Static 5's stat numbers are a bold condensed grotesk, not Geist: **unknown face**, ask the client.

**Logo:** TOMSO wordmark, heavy geometric with the M and S fused. On the site only as a 912×241 PNG (white on dark) and a
square « T » favicon; **need the SVG** (black and white versions).

**The motif:** a single thin ink line (~2 px at 1080) ending in small open ring nodes. It's in every piece: the hero's sine
wave with ringed peaks (site), tangle → straight line → spiral (static 1), stacked ellipses with nodes (static 2), hub and
spokes to bank icons (static 3), « o——o » connectors between building, TOMSO and bank (static 4), rising bars with nodes
(static 5). Icons are line pictograms (building with two windows, classical bank) in white discs.

**Layout of the statics:** logo top-left, 2–4 line caps headline in the top half, line illustration in the lower half or
right, one sentence of Geist, lime pill CTA bottom-left. Generous margins (~65–75 px at 1080), no photos, no people, no
product UI.

## 6. What we don't have

- **No product UI** anywhere on the site (no screenshots of the web interface); the two site illustrations are generic
  diagrams, one AI-generated. So the film uses the brand's line language, not invented screens. A real screen recording of
  the TOMSO interface would allow a UI beat; only if the client supplies it.
- Logo SVG; the stat font; brand guidelines document; music or sonic identity (none on the site).
