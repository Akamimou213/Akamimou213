# 3 — Creative brief: TOMSO « Le 1er » (Meta motion ad)

Built on `strategy/01_research.md` (voice, patterns, insights) and `strategy/02_ideation.md` (choices). Brand facts:
`brand_audit.md`. Every on-screen word is TOMSO's own (website), marked where adapted.

## The choices
- **Persona:** rental manager / growing plex owner in Québec, the person who runs the 1st of the month.
- **Insight:** « Le 1er du mois n'est pas une date, c'est une crise mensuelle. » Their month starts with cheques, bank
  portals and NSF before a dollar is in.
- **Angle:** the monthly crisis becomes an ordinary day: « Avec Tomso, c'est juste une journée comme toutes les autres ».
- **Pain point:** « Chèques à traiter, portails bancaires à consulter, paiements à vérifier et NSF à suivre ».
- **Motion theme:** « Le 1er »: a month calendar drawn in TOMSO's line. The 1st's cell swells with the work until it
  crowds the month; TOMSO's line with ring nodes threads through it and clears it; only the exception stays, in lime; the 1st
  shrinks back to the size of every other day.
- **Objective / KPI:** demo requests from Meta prospecting; judged on cost per booked demo (CPL as early signal), not CTR.
- **Single-minded message:** « Le 1er du mois arrive. La crise, non. »

## Hook test (only the first 4.5 s change; same body)
- **A — the date:** « LE 1ER DU MOIS MOBILISE TOUTE VOTRE ÉQUIPE. » over the swelling calendar (main).
- **B — the outcome:** opens on the lime exception: « NE GÉREZ PLUS CHAQUE PAIEMENT. GÉREZ SEULEMENT LES EXCEPTIONS. »
- **C — the bridge:** two disconnected lines, software and bank, that don't meet: « VOS SYSTÈMES NE COMMUNIQUENT PAS. » (first half of the site's line)

## Voice test
- **T:** text only, music and SFX (main).
- **V:** same picture with a calm Québec-French voice reading the lines below, captions burned in (we write « Avec TOMSO, »
  in front of the exceptions line; the rest is verbatim). 44 words ≈ 12.6 s at 3.5 words/s, so it fits with pauses.

> Le 1er du mois mobilise toute votre équipe. Chèques à traiter, portails bancaires à consulter, paiements à vérifier, NSF à
> suivre. Avec TOMSO, ne gérez plus chaque paiement : gérez seulement les exceptions. Le 1er du mois arrive. La crise, non.
> Planifiez une démo.

## Look and sound
- Look: TOMSO statics' language (`brand_audit.md` §5): flat grounds, 2 px ink lines with open ring nodes, line pictograms,
  Faculty Glyphic caps + Geist + Geist Mono, logo top-left, generous margins. No photos, no gradients, no invented product UI.
- Music: original, 120 BPM, calm and precise: soft mallets, a light pulse, warm sub; it thickens as the cell fills (tension),
  thins out when the line clears it, and resolves on the end card. Not house, not A minor.
- SFX: paper and stamp ticks as items land, a pen-line hiss when TOMSO's line draws, one soft « clear » when the cell empties,
  a resolved chord under the wordmark. SFX lead picture by up to 2 frames.

## Formats and delivery
9:16 (Reels/Stories, master), 4:5 (Feed), 1:1. FR master; EN cut from the site's EN pages. Variants: hooks A/B/C × T/V.
Poster frame that reads at 200 px. Files: `out/tomso-le-1er-<lang>-<hook><voice>-<fmt>.mp4`.
