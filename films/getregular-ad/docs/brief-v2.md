# Get Regular — v2 brief (after Athena's review of the v1 cut)

## What was wrong in v1, and the fix
| Note | Why it failed | Fix in v2 |
|---|---|---|
| Hook « This is a guess. / This is a pattern. » not understood | Abstract words over two near-identical faces; nothing on screen says "gut problem" | Open on the problem itself, then the reference's contrast device on something you can *see*: « Bloated after lunch. Again. » → split frame « Flat at nine. Bloated by two. » → « What if it isn't random? » |
| Bloating shot unreadable (hands on waistband, top-down) | Point-of-view crop, no face, no food | Side view of Maya, belly visibly round, hand on it, lunch plate beside her; the split frame does the rest |
| Doctor: flying papers look generic / like errors | Effect had no meaning in the story | No flying papers. Doctor close-ups, one question per cut; the pen taps; questions are in the subtitles only |
| The line appearing on the bed shot | Dots "flowing into the phone" read as a glitch | Shot replaced: phone close-up, she logs a meal (a simple card slides in, brand words) |
| « Stress » in the triptych, neck pain unclear | Not what the app tracks | The app's own three: **Meals · Bathroom · Symptoms** (site labels MEALS · STOOL · SYMPTOMS) |
| « A few weeks later » jumps to a field of dots | Nothing explains how the pattern appears | Show the mechanism: a 14-day grid fills day by day; the bloated afternoons light up, and the same lunch icon sits above every one of them |
| Colours off, too busy | Olive-brown haze over full-bleed busy scenes | Minimal, bright, elevated: white paper with lots of empty space, one bold vivid green shape per frame, tangerine as the signal, real black ink |

## Look v2
- **Background:** clean warm-white paper `#FAFAF7`, most of the frame empty (like the reference's white frames); one bold
  colour block per shot (a mirror, a door, a wall, a panel).
- **Anchor:** vivid leaf green `#2F7D32` (brighter cousin of the brand greens) where the reference uses cobalt.
- **Signal:** bright tangerine `#FF7A2F` (glow, the bloated days, the sun), used sparingly.
- **Ink:** `#0B0B08`. Subtitles: Inter Tight, white with a soft halo on colour, black on white.
- End card unchanged (approved).
