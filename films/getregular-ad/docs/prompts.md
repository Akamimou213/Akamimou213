## Style block (prefix every keyframe)
Vertical 9:16 full-bleed editorial illustration, contemporary gouache in a mid-century magazine style: bold flat colour
blocks, confident dry-brush texture, visible paper grain, strong value contrast, crisp shapes.

## Maya block (every shot she appears in)
Maya: early 30s, warm light-brown skin, near-black dark brown chin-length bob with loose painterly strands, defined
eyelids, dark eyes with a tiny white highlight, natural brows, rosy cheeks, softly drawn nose and lips with fine dark
line accents, a few tiny freckles; apricot-orange knit cardigan over a cream top, deep olive-green trousers.

## Palette + rules block (suffix every keyframe)
Palette strictly: deep olive green #3A4B1C dominant, cream paper #FEFFF5, apricot #E58A4E, ink #1E1E1C, warm skin.
Calm area in the lower-middle for subtitles. No text, no logos, no gradients, no 3D, no photorealism.

## Lessons from the style tests
- v1 (half body, soft storybook face) read as a different illustrator. Fix: very tight framing (head and shoulder fill
  the frame), defined eyelids + eye highlights + fine line accents, near-black hair. The reference crops close and lets
  shapes run off the edge.
- Reuse the reference's devices with our palette: flat horizon band, a flat sun circle partly hidden by the figure,
  over-the-shoulder glances.
