# Director's brief — Get Regular, 30 s (restart)

## The film in one line
Maya's gut has felt off for months, and at the doctor she can't answer three simple questions. Logging as it happens turns
"could be stress" into answers. The viewer should leave thinking "that's me at the doctor" and see the app as the fix.

## Why a restart
The first cut (`share_9_1.mp4`, 51 s) told the story in a game frame: "LV.1 gut guesser", "Level up!", numbered steps,
UI badges and a warm sepia look. The client didn't like the style and found the story long. This version:
- takes the **grammar** of the reference (`Video-96789_1.mp4`, a 5-minute gouache-illustrated supplement ad): a cobalt,
  terracotta and cream palette, paper grain, framed panels on white paper, centred serif lines, and a hard cut every ~2 s;
- keeps the **same narrator**: every line is a take from the first cut, re-cut from 51 s to 26 s (no new VO needed);
- drops the gamification (levels, badges, "Time to level up", step numbers) and the "give it a few weeks" step.

## Inputs
- Subject: Get Regular (iOS gut-health app). Real assets: logo SVG, app home screen and copy from getregular.com (captured
  2026-10-29), the narrator's VO from the first cut.
- Goal: paid social / app-install creative, 9:16 first (4:5 second). Judge it on cost per install that logs in week 1, not CTR.
- Duration: 30 s (VO 26 s + 3.6 s end card).
- Captions: the VO lines are the on-screen serif lines (the reference's treatment), so the film reads with the sound off.
- Music: original, synthesized; quiet under the VO.

## Look
- Palette (sampled from the reference): paper #F3F1EA, cobalt #1A44B8, deep cobalt #0E36A0, navy #0B0E2E,
  terracotta #D9682E, peach #DEB49C, olive #727242.
- Type: EB Garamond (OFL) for every line, like the reference's serif; the real Get Regular wordmark (SVG) on the end card.
- Texture: gouache fills with brush streaks, rough panel edges, paper grain over everything.
- The recurring character: Maya as a navy silhouette with her gut drawn as a glowing terracotta line: knotted in the hook,
  calm in the resolution.
- Banned: badges, levels, HUD chrome, corner labels, emoji, fades from black.

## Honest limits
The reference was painted (image models plus image-to-video). This film is drawn in code: flat gouache shapes, silhouettes
and objects rather than painted faces. The palette, grain, framing, type and cut rhythm match; the painterly detail does not.
