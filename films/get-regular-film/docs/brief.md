# Director's brief — Get Regular, 30 s

## The film in one line
Maya's gut has felt off for months, and at the doctor she can't answer three simple questions. Logging as it happens turns
"could be stress" into answers. The viewer should leave thinking "that's me at the doctor" and see the app as the fix.

## Versions
1. First cut (`share_9_1.mp4`, 51 s): game framing ("LV.1 gut guesser", "Level up!"), warm sepia. Too long; the style was off.
2. Restart v1 (2026-10-29, morning): the reference's look copied (cobalt gouache) with Maya replaced by a faceless silhouette.
   Client note: it should be **Get Regular branded**; the reference is inspiration only; **bring the character back**.
3. **This version:** the reference's storytelling grammar, Get Regular's own look, and Maya and the doctor drawn in code from
   the first film's designs.

## What we take from the reference (`Video-96789_1.mp4`), and what we don't
- Take: an illustrated story led by a recurring woman; one action per shot; a hard cut every ~2.5 s; the line centred under the
  picture; full-bleed shots alternating with shots on paper; callbacks where the same scene returns changed.
- Don't take: its cobalt palette, serif captions, painted textures or content.

## Brand (getregular.com, captured 2026-10-29)
- Grounds: warm near-black #0E0C08 with soft moss glows (the site hero), alternating with cream paper #F3EEE4.
- Colour: moss #555F2D / #374119, sage #9AA77A, the app's teal #2D4A47 and clay "Nutrition" brown #6E3B23, clay #A0532E as the
  accent (the gut line), the app's thin green ring #5E8A55 around circles.
- Type: Inter Tight Light (the site's headline face, OFL) for every line; italic for the accent word ("Gut feelings. *Confirmed.*",
  like "Your gut is *talking*."); DM Sans for the CTA (the site's UI face); the real logo SVG.

## Characters (from the first film)
- **Maya:** dark-brown wavy bob with a side part, mustard cardigan, white top, black trousers. Moods: worried, blank, smile, calm.
- **The doctor:** silver bob, round glasses, white coat over a sage top, stethoscope, clipboard. Moods: listening, smile.
- Drawn in code: flat editorial shapes with light paper texture, simple faces (dot eyes, brows, a mouth). They blink and breathe.

## Inputs and deliverables
- VO: the narrator's own takes from the first cut, re-cut to 26 s (no new recording).
- 9:16 master and 4:5 cut; lines burned in (they are the VO, so the film reads with the sound off); original score.
- Paid social: judge it on cost per install that logs in week 1, not CTR.
