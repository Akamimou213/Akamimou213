# Style guide — reference grammar in the Get Regular look

Reference: `Video-96789_1.mp4` (296 s, 720×1280, 24 fps). `refscan.sh`: 109 hard cuts, so the average shot is **2.7 s**; VO-led.
We take its grammar, not its look or content.

| Reference grammar | Ours, in Get Regular's look |
|---|---|
| A recurring woman carries the story | Maya (first film's design): worried → blank → smiling with her report → calm |
| Full-bleed shots alternate with shots on paper | near-black with moss glows (the site hero) alternates with cream paper |
| One action per shot | stamp, questions fly, rise, the thought empties, write and slide off, tap, circles fill, drop, ticks, flow |
| Object shots: triptych, grid, plate and cutlery | triptych (clock · dots · plate) in moss, teal and clay; the grid as four app-style circles with thin green rings |
| The same scene returns changed | the doctor two-shot (questions → answers), Maya (knot → calm), the calendar (marks → logs into a report) |
| Lines centred under the picture, sentence case | Inter Tight Light 62 px/1080: white with a soft shadow on dark, ink on cream; figures fade into the ground before the line |
| Hard cuts on VO phrases | all 12 cuts are hard cuts on word starts |

Palette tokens and texture strengths live in `timeline.json` (`palette`, `textures`).
