# Director's brief: Appfound explainer film

**One-sentence idea.** Your ads work; your users leak out between the install and the payment, and Appfound plugs every
leak, then feeds and scales only what pays.

**Viewer outcome.** A founder of a consumer or subscription app can say, after 64 s: "Installs aren't the goal. Appfound
fixes the steps between the ad and the payment, in three phases, starting with a free Growth Plan."

- **Subject + source:** Appfound (https://appfound.vercel.app/, source in `appfound/` in this repo). All copy is verbatim from
  the site unless `SOURCES.md` marks it as ours.
- **Audience and what they already believe:** founders and heads of growth at consumer and subscription apps, typically
  spending $2K–$50K a month on ads. They believe more installs, or a cheaper CPI, means growth.
- **The one question the film answers, in their words:** "Why am I paying for installs that never turn into revenue?"
- **The hook (the belief that turns out wrong):** installs = growth. Opening line: "INSTALLS DON'T PAY." (site H1).
- **Metaphor test:** the funnel is shown as the real sequence of stages a user passes (Ad > Store > Onboarding > Paywall >
  Paying), the same strip the site's hero animates. No bucket or pipe metaphor; dots are users.
- **No results by design:** no client numbers, percentages or spend figures appear. The only numbers are the service
  facts the site states (30–40 ads a month, 90 days, 48 hours) and the process timings.
- **Length / formats:** 64 s (32 bars at 120 BPM). 16:9 master, then 9:16 and 1:1 recompositions from the same timeline
  (9:16 turns the funnel into a vertical stack instead of shrinking it).
- **References:** ref A (Laminar "flow-1", 74 s): an agent ball lays a track of labelled blocks, cards hang under blocks,
  coral highlights, a dark grid, and a bare wordmark at the end. Ref B ("Lenny's Bots", 13 s): white ground, wordmark, and a
  scrolling list of pills with icons. We took the grammar from both, never the content (`docs/style_guide.md`).
- **Sound:** an original 120 BPM bed plus real CC0 recordings for every effect, placed from the timeline. No voice-over:
  the type carries the film, because feeds autoplay muted. On-screen lines also ship as `captions.srt`.

## Story arc (Question → Model → Proof → Turn → Payoff)
| Beat | Time | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Question | 0–9.25 | INSTALLS DON'T PAY. Users stream in; a head lays the funnel; leaks open at Store, Onboarding, Paywall; few reach Paying. "More installs won't fix a funnel that leaks." |
| Why | 9.5–16.25 | Four founder quotes from the site; each flashes the stage it describes. |
| Model | 17.0–23.6 | The one paying user turns lime and opens Appfound's blue. Logo, who it is for. "Every lever between the ad and the payment." |
| Proof (mechanism) | 24–41.5 | Three phases, six services, each landing on the stage it fixes: Tracking, ASO & funnel, Creative, Paid UA, SEO, AI visibility. |
| Turn | 41.5–44 | "Scale only what pays." Paying fills lime. |
| Process | 44–55.5 | The first 90 days, step by step: 9 steps with your time, then the guarantee. |
| Payoff | 55.5–64 | The operator. "INSTALLS DON'T PAY. SUBSCRIBERS DO." over the same funnel, every user paying. Free Growth Plan, clicked. |
