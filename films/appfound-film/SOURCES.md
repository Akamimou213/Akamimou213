## Numbers
There are no results, percentages or spend figures in the film, by design. The only numbers are service facts the site
states: 30–40 tested ads a month, the 90-day sprint and its week ranges, Day 0 and Day 90, 48 hours.

## Illustrative motion, not data
Users are dots. How many fall at each leak (`flow.leak` in `timeline.json`), how many pay, the ad-card spend bars, the A/B
winners and the search and AI results are motion that explains the model, the same way the site's "Illustration" panels do.
They are not client data and carry no numbers.
