# Sound credits

Every sound effect in the film is a real recording, not synthesized. All are CC0 (public domain): credit is not required but given.
The music bed is original, synthesized in numpy by `.claude/skills/motion-reel/scripts/music.py` (no samples).

Sources were picked from the pinned library manifest of [opus-sound-layer](https://github.com/Bodila51/opus-sound-layer) (`scripts/library.json`) and downloaded as audio files only; none of that repo's code was run.
