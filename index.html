Principles

Paid growth is full of advice that doesn't survive a spreadsheet. Here's what does.

Five rules I use to decide where the next dollar goes, and what I'll push back on before spending it.

I

Platform numbers are a claim, not a result.

Meta, Google and TikTok each grade their own homework, and their windows overlap. Add them up and you often get more customers than you actually have.

I read platform results next to backend or CRM data, check the attribution window, and split new from returning customers before moving a dollar.

II

Optimize for the conversion that pays you.

A click is not a customer and a lead is not a sale. Cheap top-of-funnel events are easy to buy and easy to over-report.

Campaigns optimize toward purchases, qualified leads, booked calls or paid subscriptions: the event closest to revenue that still has enough volume to learn from.

III

Creative is the targeting now.

With broad audiences and automated bidding, the ad itself decides who stops scrolling. Swapping audiences rarely fixes a weak message.

So creative gets a strategy: researched angles, specific hooks, and tests that change one thing at a time.

IV

Tracking comes before scale.

Scaling on broken conversion data just buys more of the wrong thing, faster. It's the most expensive mistake in paid acquisition and the easiest to miss.

Conversion APIs, server-side events and CRM feedback get checked against the backend before the budget goes up.

V

AI speeds up the work, not the judgment.

I use Claude Code and Codex for research, creative development and analysis. That means more angles explored and faster reads on the data.

The decisions about your money are still made by a person who reads the numbers and owns the outcome.

FAQ

Questions people ask before the first call.

Who do you usually work with?

D2C and e-commerce brands that need profitable purchases, apps and marketplaces that need paying customers, local businesses that need calls and bookings, and B2B teams that need qualified leads and booked demos.

Which channels do you run?

Meta, Google (Search, Performance Max, Demand Gen and Shopping), TikTok, Snapchat, LinkedIn and Reddit, with SEO and AI visibility alongside the paid plan.

What budget do I need?

There's no fixed minimum. I've run local campaigns from €10 a day and managed $50K a month for a super app. On the call we check whether your budget can buy enough conversions to learn from, and if it can't, where it's better spent.

Do you make the ads too?

I lead the creative strategy: research, angles, hooks, ad copy, UGC briefs and static concepts. Filming and editing are done by your team or the creators we brief, and I read the results and write the next round.

How do you report results?

Weekly and monthly, always with a recommendation. Platform-attributed numbers sit next to backend or CRM data, and everything is tied to the conversion you actually earn money from.

How soon will I see results?

Audit findings and tracking fixes come first. Paid tests need enough conversions before they can be read, and SEO compounds over months. I won't promise numbers before I've seen your data.

Can I hire you just for SEO?

Yes. SEO and AI visibility works as a standalone service, covering technical fixes, local SEO, content and structured data, or it can run alongside paid so both channels share the same keyword and conversion data.

Can you work with our in-house team or agency?

Yes. Most of my work has been alongside founders, creative teams and sales teams. I can own the whole system or plug into the levers you need help with.
