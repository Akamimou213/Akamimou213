Principles

Paid growth is full of advice that doesn't survive a spreadsheet. Here's what does.

Five rules I use to decide where the next dollar goes, and what I'll push back on before spending it.

I

Platform numbers are a claim, not a result.

Meta, Google and TikTok each grade their own homework, and their windows overlap. Add them up and you often get more customers than you actually have.

I read platform results next to MMP or backend data, check the attribution window, and split new from returning customers before moving a dollar.

II

Optimize for the event that pays you.

An install is not a customer and a lead is not a sale. Cheap top-of-funnel events are easy to buy and easy to over-report.

Campaigns optimize toward first orders, trial starts, subscriptions or booked demos: the event closest to revenue that still has enough volume to learn.

III

Creative is the targeting now.

With broad audiences and automated bidding, the ad itself decides who stops scrolling. Swapping audiences rarely fixes a weak message.

So creative gets a strategy: researched angles, specific hooks, and tests that change one thing at a time.

IV

Tracking comes before scale.

Scaling on broken events just buys more of the wrong thing, faster. It's the most expensive mistake in paid acquisition and the easiest to miss.

CAPI, server-side events, SDK events and SKAN get checked against the backend before the budget goes up.

V

AI speeds up the work, not the judgment.

I use Claude Code and Codex for research, creative development and analysis. It means more angles explored and faster reads on the data.

The decisions about your money are still made by a person who reads the numbers and owns the outcome.
